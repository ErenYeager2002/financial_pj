# 07 — Foundation, supporting, and publication gates

**What to build:** Foundation file Skills retain a compact general-purpose experience, supporting Skills use diagnostic or conversational entry types, and draft or disabled business Skills cannot be republished without a registered experience.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Controlled registry and canonical execution routes.

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [x] xlsx, docx, pdf, and pptx are treated as foundation file Skills.
- [x] env-doctor and task-clarifier resolve to supporting experience types.
- [x] Business publication validation rejects a missing or unknown experience key.
- [x] Disabled business Skills remain unavailable until their experience is registered.
- [x] Classification and publication rules have automated tests.
